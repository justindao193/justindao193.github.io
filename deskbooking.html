The problem

“It says full, but the seats are empty.”

It started in our team chat. People could not find a desk, even on days the office looked half empty.

40 people.
9 desks.

Problem statement

Team members struggled to plan office visits because desk availability and who owned each booking were unclear, especially when plans changed.

The existing tool

Four friction points in Smart Office

01 · SetupSix fields before a single desk
02 · Time slotsPartial hours invite double booking
03 · VisibilityBooked desks show no name
04 · CancellingTwo steps and a reason no one reads

Research

A small group, not the whole team

I pulled the booking data from IT and analyzed it with Copilot.

10

people made 72% of all no-shows

57%

of bookings were no-shows

21%

came from outside the team

4

pairs of double bookings

Leadership saw the report and agreed: we needed an alternative.

How might we help the team plan office days with confidence, and make sure booked desks get used?

Direction

A shared sheet, or a purpose-built app

My manager proposed a shared Excel sheet. I kept its openness, and proposed an app that enforces the rules on its own.

Excel sheetApp
NamesTyped in by handPulled from the booking
RulesAnyone can edit or deleteEnforced automatically
UpkeepNeeds an ownerMaintains itself

Design

Each need became one feature

I walked the journey step by step and asked what the team needed at each point.

  1. 01Plan a visitNeedAvailabilityFeatureWeek view
  2. 02See who bookedNeedVisibilityFeatureNames shown
  3. 03Book a deskNeedSimplicityFeatureEasy booking
  4. 04Change plansNeedFlexibilityFeatureEasy cancel
  5. 05Arrive and sit downNeedReliabilityFeatureAuto-release

Iteration

From sketch to final in four rounds

Copilot built each version from my sketch and rules. My job was editing: removing noise until it was clear where to look.

The solution

Book, check in or cancel from one page

Four pages in the Vale brand: Book a desk, Who’s in, My bookings and Insights.

01 · Plan an office day

See free desks at a glance

Full-day bookings match how people work and remove double booking.

02 · Manage a booking

All your bookings in one place

See upcoming days and past visits, and manage each one from the same list.

03 · Identify the holder

A name on every desk

No more guessing who has a desk, or who to ask.

What I learned

Speed is not the same as certainty

UsersTest with people sooner

AI did most early testing. Two people saw it near the end.

Platform limits should shape the design, not surprise it.
